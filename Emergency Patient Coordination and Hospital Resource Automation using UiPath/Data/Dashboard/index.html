How each emergency episode is automated

Every step below is performed by the RPA bot. Only the two amber HUMAN steps need a person — a doctor's sign-off. Everything else, intake to discharge, is unattended coordination across the hospital's systems.

01
Verify documents
ID, insurance, consent, medical report
02
Check bed
Department, ventilator-linked where needed
03
Check ventilator
Allocated with the ICU bed
04
Check blood
Group and units against inventory
05
Assign care team
On-call consultant by load; + surgeon & anaesthetist if surgical
06
Readiness score
Four checks, 25% each
07
Consolidated status
One case summary
08
Approve admission
Authorised sign-off before any resource is committed
HUMAN
09
Book theatre
Operating-theatre slot for surgical cases
10
Reserve resources
Bed, ventilator, blood, doctor, OT — rollback on failure
11
Confirm admission
Bed occupied, billing account opened
12
Accrue charges
Bed & doctor fees every day, unattended
13
Certify discharge
Attending doctor confirms the patient is fit to leave
HUMAN
14
Process discharge
Final bill, insurance claim, discharge summary, free resources
15
Follow-up & close
Reminder to patient & GP, episode closed
